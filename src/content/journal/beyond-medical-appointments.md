## 1. Research Question

How do individuals organize their everyday environments and social commitments to accommodate the ongoing, non-clinical demands of chronic condition management?

## 2. Why This Matters

Healthcare systems interact with individuals primarily during acute crises or scheduled outpatient reviews—infrequent touchpoints lasting 15 to 30 minutes. However, chronic illness is experienced across the remaining 8,700 hours of the year. When services and digital products are designed exclusively around clinical metrics, they often misunderstand the contextual trade-offs individuals must make between adhering to recommendations and sustaining their livelihoods, families, and emotional wellbeing.

## 3. Scope and Approach

* **Approach:** Secondary literature review of qualitative sociological literature, patient-reported outcome studies, and human-centred technology frameworks.
