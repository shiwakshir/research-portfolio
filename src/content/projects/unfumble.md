## The problem

People lose time when a portal rejects a document or a photo, and many online converters require an upload of sensitive files.

## What it does

A person describes a multi-step file task in ordinary English, and Unfumble builds a working tool for that task. Smart Fix checks a rejected file against the portal rules, reports what passes and fails, and repairs what it can. File processing runs in the browser. An AI key is optional.
