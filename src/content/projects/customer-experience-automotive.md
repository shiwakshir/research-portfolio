## Project Overview

Qualitative customer experience research examining key transition moments across automotive shopping and long-term vehicle ownership.

### Role & Execution
I developed in-depth interview guides for different stages of the shopping and ownership journey.

### Research Scope & Methodology
* **Journey Stage Protocols:** Structured interview guides tailored to distinct emotional and practical milestones—from initial digital discovery and dealership interactions to vehicle handover and ongoing post-purchase servicing.
* **Uncovering Hidden Friction:** Identifying unexpressed customer hesitations, information gaps, and service disconnects that standard customer satisfaction surveys fail to capture.
* **Strategic CX Recommendations:** Providing actionable guidance for enhancing physical and digital service touchpoints across the ownership lifecycle.
