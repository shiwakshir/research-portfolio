## Project Overview

**BRIEF** is an independent agentic system designed to help researchers, consultants and product teams stress-test research plans before committing budget and fieldwork hours.

### The Problem
Flawed research designs often pass through review unnoticed. Common risks—such as ambiguous participant recruitment parameters, double-barrelled survey questions, unvalidated behavioural assumptions, or sample size misalignments—routinely lead to wasted client investment and unreliable insights.

### What I Designed & Built
I designed and built this 11-agent Flask application independently:
* **Multi-Agent Evaluation:** 11 specialized autonomous agents inspect different dimensions of the submitted research brief (e.g., sample representation, objective clarity, methodological fit, cognitive bias risks, and ethical parameters).
* **Enterprise AI Infrastructure:** The application integrates **Microsoft Foundry** and **Azure AI Search** for contextual methodology indexing and reference retrieval.
* **Quantified Confidence:** Synthesizes multi-agent evaluations into an actionable confidence score from 0 to 100 alongside specific remediation recommendations.
