## Context and Philosophy

In six years of leading UX and market research initiatives, the most common pitfall observed is methodological isolation—conducting qualitative interviews without checking their prevalence, or launching quantitative surveys whose questions were drafted without genuine qualitative grounding.

This project outlines an institutional research model that ensures discovery informs measurement, and measurement informs validation.

## Methodological Phases

### Phase 1: Exploratory Discovery
* Semi-structured depth interviews focusing on unprompted user mental models.
* Contextual observation to identify behavioral workarounds and unspoken pain points.
* Thematic qualitative coding to identify recurring patterns.

### Phase 2: Quantitative Verification
* Statistically sound survey instruments designed around qualitatively validated terminology.
* Feature trade-off analyses (MaxDiff / Conjoint where appropriate) to measure real preferences versus unconstrained wish lists.
* Behavioral data cross-referencing.

### Phase 3: Evidence Triangulation & Strategic Synthesis
* Explicit confidence scoring on findings.
* Translation of complex behavioral findings into concrete product recommendations and service principles.
