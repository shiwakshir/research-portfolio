## The problem

Static surveys cannot follow up on an answer, and human-moderated interviews cost more. ChoiceGap tests a middle route that keeps the researcher in control.

## What it does

A researcher starts from a plain-language brief and hypotheses. ChoiceGap prepares cited background research, concept stimuli, a survey and an interview guide. After approval, participants complete a survey, a spoken or typed interview, or both. Follow-up questions stay within the approved topics.

## Current status

I built ChoiceGap with Meta Muse models for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. I tested it with fictional material and simulated rules, not with a validated participant study. Its results do not establish why a person made a choice.
