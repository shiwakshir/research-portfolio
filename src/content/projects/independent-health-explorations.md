## Project Overview

Managing a long-term or fluctuating health condition is a continuous, dynamic negotiation. Individuals must balance medication schedules, symptom management, rest cycles, and unpredictable physical capacity against workplace responsibilities, family obligations, and personal aspirations.

This independent research exploration investigates how digital tools can act as quiet, respectful scaffolding rather than demanding taskmasters.

## Research Questions Under Exploration

1. **The Burden of Interaction:** How much cognitive friction does manual symptom tracking introduce during periods of reduced capacity?
2. **Mental Models of Progress:** Why do streak-based gamification metrics consistently fail users navigating episodic conditions?
3. **Compassionate Interface Principles:** How might an interface adapt its density and notification frequency based on user-indicated capacity?

## Methodology and Guardrails

In line with our commitment to research integrity:
* This project is pursued as an independent research exploration.
* It does not represent clinical trials or medical device claims.
* Findings will be published iteratively through our research journal series once literature reviews and qualitative analyses are completed.
